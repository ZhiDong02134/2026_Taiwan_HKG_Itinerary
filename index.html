Operating Rules · 旅行規則

Move efficiently · 交通Walk/public transit when direct. For 3 people, take a taxi/app car when it saves ~15+ min, removes an awkward transfer, protects a booking, or avoids dragging luggage.
Don’t queue · 排隊Default food line cutoff: ~10–15 min. Use the named nearby backup rather than sacrificing the route for a marginally better restaurant.
Book lightly · 預約Reserve when it is easy and free. Avoid prepayment, non-refundable deposits or card guarantees unless the experience genuinely requires it.
Protect energy · 體力The active days and booked recovery sessions are already sufficient. Skip optional gym/bodywork when it would compromise sleep, hikes or fixed experiences.
