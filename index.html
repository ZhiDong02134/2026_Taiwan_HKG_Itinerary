Travel Tips & Wellness Playbook · 旅行與恢復策略

  • • Gym Routine: Planned maintenance lifts Dec 1 / 10 / 12. Dec 4 cable wakeboarding replaces the gym session; Dec 7 stays active-recovery only after Alishan + Cijin. Keep lifts ~45–60 min with 2–3 reps in reserve; no failure/high-volume legs. The goal is maintaining routine without compromising walking, hikes, sleep or booked experiences.
  • • Wellness Diversity Strategy: Dec 1 Thermal sulfur soak (Grand View) ➔ Dec 2 Thai deep stretching (NARA, NT$1,299) ➔ Dec 3 Scalp & warm SPA shampoo (Pine Foot, NT$1,290, flexible) ➔ Dec 7 Taiwanese full-body acupressure (Left Foot/Right Foot, NT$1,700).
  • • Massage Value · 按摩性價比: Keep the trip Taiwan-heavy. NARA is NT$1,299 for 120 min and Left Foot/Right Foot is NT$1,700 for 100 min; reputable TST options are materially pricier per minute (Rendezvous 60-min Chinese body massage HK$346; Tai Pan 50-min full-body HK$348). Taiwan gets the fixed bodywork slots; Hong Kong remains fatigue-triggered only.
  • • Hong Kong Fatigue-Triggered Massage · 香港疲勞備案: Tai Pan Reflexology Parlour (大班公館) at 83 Nathan Rd is almost next door to Sasa Inn. Use it only when the group genuinely wants recovery after a long walking day. Rendezvous (足藝舍) remains the stronger dedicated-bodywork alternative; no fixed HK massage booking.
  • • Single-Use Amenities: Bring toothbrush/razor. Taiwan hotels generally do not pre-place selected disposable amenities; Hong Kong hotels cannot provide disposable plastic toiletries free.
  • • Octopus in Apple Wallet: Issue via the Octopus App for Tourists using US Apple Pay before arriving in Hong Kong.
  • • Cheap + Convenient Transport · 便宜方便優先: Walk when the leg is short and weather is fine; use MRT/TRA/THSR/bus/tram/ferry when direct. For a group of 3, take a car when it saves ~15+ minutes, removes an awkward transfer, protects a booking, or avoids dragging luggage. Do not pay for a car just because it exists. · 短程且天氣舒服就走路；捷運／台鐵／高鐵／公車／電車／渡輪順路就優先搭。3人同行時，若搭車可省約15分鐘以上、少一次麻煩轉乘、保住預約時間或免拖行李，就值得叫車。
  • • Food Queue / Reservation Rule: Walk-ins get ~10–15 min by default. Use a reservation only when it is easy and free/cancellable with no prepayment, deposit or card guarantee. A named same-area fallback beats a “better” restaurant that requires backtracking.
  • • Restaurant Routing Rule · 餐廳路線原則: Route efficiency beats a marginally better restaurant. Default queue cutoff is ~10–15 minutes; if the line is longer, use the same-neighborhood backup rather than backtracking. Reserve only when the process is simple and free—skip prepayment, non-refundable deposits, or card guarantees unless a truly special meal justifies it. · 餐廳以順路優先；排隊約10–15分鐘就換同區備案，不為了小幅提升品質折返。預約只選簡單免費的流程。
  • • Taiwan Taxi Order · 台灣叫車順序: For car-worthy hops, compare Uber with 55688 / LINE GO once the Taiwan number is active and choose the best ETA + price. When the price gap is small, prefer an app-dispatched ride for the recorded driver/route. Official taxi stands and street yellow taxis remain useful backups—confirm the meter starts and skip any unsolicited fixed-price quote. Full-day private cars are exceptions, not the default.
  • • Hong Kong Taxi Order · 香港叫車順序: Walk/MTR/tram/bus/ferry first. When a car wins, use a red metered taxi from the street/official stand; DiDi Taxi or Uber Meter Taxi are useful dispatch backups. Compare premium Uber categories only when pricing is close. A private 7-seater is for unusual luggage/capacity needs—not normal city hops.
  • • Airport Express Kowloon: The ride between HKG Airport and Kowloon Station takes ~22 min. For 3 adults with suitcases, use the official red metered-taxi stand for the ~5-min ride to Sasa Inn at 87 Nathan Rd; there is usually no reason to pay an Uber/private-car premium for this hop.

Taiwan Number + Savings · 台灣門號省錢設定

Useful setup, not coupon-chasing. Keep the trip efficient.

PREP NOW
Before JFK · 出發前Install OPENPOINT + My FamiPort from the U.S. App Store now and keep your U.S. Apple ID region unchanged. OPENPOINT/uniopen NEW registration currently requires a +886 Taiwan mobile number, so install now but register only after the local number is active.
Arrival · 抵台Low-friction default for a U.S. visa-exempt traveler: activate one Chunghwa Telecom (CHT) 10-day 4G tourist SIM/eSIM on the group-admin phone after arrival (current plan NT$500, unlimited data + NT$100 voice). Bring passport + a qualifying U.S. government photo ID such as a driver’s license. Taiwan Mobile’s current 10-day NT$450 option is cheaper but its published foreign secondary-ID list is narrower; use it only if your documents qualify. If all 3 travelers need paid Taiwan data anyway, separate local-number lines can be reasonable; do not buy extra lines solely to chase coupons.
Protein Deals · 蛋白質補給Check FamilyMart 隨買跨店取 / 7-ELEVEN member offers for soy milk, milk, yogurt, eggs, chicken or other items you would buy anyway. Buy multi-packs only when the live discount is meaningful.
Payment · 付款Do not waste time forcing Taiwan-only wallets. FamilyMart cross-store items can be bought through supported store/FamiPort flows; use normal in-store payment when that is simpler.

Protein Strategy · 蛋白質策略

Aim for ~150 g protein/person/day without sacrificing local food. Rough operating split: 35–45 g first meal + 40–50 g lunch + 45–60 g dinner + a 15–30 g top-up only when needed. Prioritize local beef, chicken, pork, seafood, eggs, tofu/soy and dim sum; share starch-heavy snacks/desserts instead of making them the meal base. · 每人每日目標約150g蛋白質，同時保留在地美食。大致可抓首餐35–45g＋午餐40–50g＋晚餐45–60g；不足時再補15–30g。優先牛、雞、豬、海鮮、蛋、豆腐／豆製品與點心；澱粉型小吃與甜點以分食嚐鮮為主。

Taiwan fallback · 台灣補給：tea eggs, unsweetened soy milk, ready-to-eat chicken/lean meat, yogurt/milk, tofu/edamame when available. Hong Kong fallback · 香港補給：eggs, milk/soy milk, yogurt, tuna/ready meat or supermarket prepared protein depending on what the nearby shop stocks. Exact products vary by branch—buy what is freshest and easiest rather than chasing a specific SKU.

Food Routing Standard · 餐飲動線規則

Named stops only: every planned restaurant/snack target and every food backup is explicitly named inside that day's timeline. Core selections were re-screened on 2026-09-09 for independent review depth, recent traveler/local feedback, current hours and route fit; influencer/sponsored-list presence alone was not accepted as evidence. No review platform can prove every individual review is organic, so this is a fraud-resistant screening standard—not a guarantee about every reviewer.

Operational rule: route efficiency > marginal food upside. Default food queue cutoff is ~10–15 min. If a named primary fails, use the named same-area fallback shown in the timeline; otherwise skip rather than backtrack. Recheck opening hours, temporary closures and reservations 48–72h before each city/day.
